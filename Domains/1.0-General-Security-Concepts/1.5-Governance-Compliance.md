## 🏛️ Governance & Policy Framework
Governance is the set of responsibilities and practices exercised by the board and executive management to provide strategic direction and ensure that objectives are achieved.

### 1. The Policy Hierarchy
* **Policy:** High-level statements of intent (e.g., "All employees must use MFA"). These are mandatory.
* **Standard:** Specific mandatory requirements for hardware/software (e.g., "All laptops must run Windows 11").
* **Procedure:** Step-by-step instructions for a task (e.g., "How to reset a password").
* **Guideline:** Recommended but non-mandatory advice.

### 2. Key Organizational Policies
* **Acceptable Use Policy (AUP):** Defines what a user can and cannot do on company systems.
* **Separation of Duties:** Ensuring that one person doesn't have too much power (e.g., the person who requests a check cannot be the one who signs it).
* **Least Privilege:** Giving users only the minimum access necessary to do their jobs.

## ⚖️ Compliance & Regulations
Compliance is the act of following laws, regulations, and industry standards. Failure to comply leads to fines, lawsuits, and loss of reputation.

| Regulation | Full Name | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **GDPR** | General Data Protection Regulation | Privacy rights for citizens of the European Union (EU). |
| **HIPAA** | Health Insurance Portability & Accountability Act | Security and privacy of healthcare data (USA). |
| **PCI-DSS** | Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard | Security for credit card transactions (Industry standard, not law). |
| **SOX** | Sarbanes-Oxley Act | Financial reporting and auditing for public companies (USA). |

## 🎲 Risk Management
Risk is the probability of a threat actor exploiting a vulnerability. 

### 1. Risk Assessment Types
* **Quantitative:** Uses numbers and money (e.g., "This breach will cost us $50,000").
    * **SLE (Single Loss Expectancy):** Asset Value x Exposure Factor.
    * **ARO (Annualized Rate of Occurrence):** How often it happens per year.
    * **ALE (Annualized Loss Expectancy):** SLE x ARO.
* **Qualitative:** Uses descriptions (e.g., "High," "Medium," "Low") based on impact and likelihood.

### 2. Risk Response Strategies
When you find a risk, you have four choices:
1. **Mitigate:** Reduce the risk (e.g., install a firewall).
2. **Transfer:** Shift the risk to someone else (e.g., buy cyber insurance).
3. **Accept:** Do nothing because the cost of the fix is higher than the risk itself.
4. **Avoid:** Stop the activity that creates the risk (e.g., stop using a dangerous piece of software).

## 📂 Key Documentation
* **Business Impact Analysis (BIA):** Identifies critical systems and the impact of their failure.
* **Service Level Agreement (SLA):** A contract with a vendor defining expected uptime/performance.
* **Memorandum of Understanding (MOU):** A non-binding agreement between two parties to work together.

---

## 🔑 Key Acronyms
| Acronym | Full Name | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DPO** | Data Protection Officer | Person responsible for overseeing data privacy (required by GDPR). |
| **PIA** | Privacy Impact Assessment | An analysis of how PII (Personally Identifiable Information) is handled. |
| **RPO** | Recovery Point Objective | How much data can we afford to lose? (Time-based). |
| **RTO** | Recovery Time Objective | How quickly must we be back up and running? |
