# 1.2 Summarize Fundamental Security Concepts

> **Exam Domain:** 1.0 General Security Concepts  
> **Status:** 🟢 Complete

---

## 🔺 The CIA Triad
The core of all security programs. Every security decision is made to protect one of these three legs.
* **Confidentiality:** Ensuring only authorized parties can access data.
    * *Controls:* Encryption, Access Control Lists (ACLs), MFA.
* **Integrity:** Ensuring data has not been tampered with or altered.
    * *Controls:* Hashing (MD5, SHA), Digital Signatures, Version Control.
* **Availability:** Ensuring systems and data are accessible when needed.
    * *Controls:* Redundancy (RAID), Backups, DDoS protection, UPS/Generators.

---
## 🤝 Non-Repudiation
The assurance that someone cannot deny the validity of something. In digital terms, it means a sender cannot deny sending a message, and a recipient cannot deny receiving it.
* **How it's achieved:** Primarily through **Digital Signatures** (which combine Hashing + Asymmetric Encryption).

---

## 🔑 AAA Framework (The Triple A)
This is the process of identifying a user and what they can do.

1. **Authentication:** Proving you are who you say you are (Passwords, Biometrics).
2. **Authorization:** Determining what you are allowed to do (Permissions, Read/Write access).
3. **Accounting:** Tracking what you did and when (Log files, Audit trails).

---

## 🛡️ Zero Trust Architecture
A security model based on the principle: **"Never trust, always verify."**
* **Key Components:**
    * **Control Plane:** The policy-making part of the network (decides who gets in).
    * **Data Plane:** The part that actually carries the traffic (enforces the decision).
    * **Adaptive Identity:** Checking context (Is this login from a new country? At 3 AM?).

---

## 🚧 Physical Security & Deception
Security doesn't just happen on a screen. 

### Physical Controls
* **Bollards:** Concrete/metal posts to stop vehicles from ramming buildings.
* **Access Control Vestibule (Mantraps):** A two-door entrance where the first door must close before the second opens.
* **Sensors:** Infrared (heat), Microwave (motion), and Ultrasonic (sound waves).

### Deception & Disruption
* **Honeypot:** A single decoy server designed to distract and study attackers.
* **Honeynet:** A whole network of decoys.
* **Honeyfile/Honeytoken:** Fake files or data (like a "Passwords.txt" file) that trigger an alert the second they are opened.
